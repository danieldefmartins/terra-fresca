Who we are

A trading house, not a middleman

Terra Fresca buys at the farm gate and takes ownership of every container it moves. The grower is paid for the fruit. The importer buys from one seller. Nobody in between is guessing at quality, price, or timing.

Our track record

Fifteen years in the same valleys

Terra Fresca has been trading Brazilian produce for over fifteen years, with more than thirty partner farms and retail operations across Minas Gerais and São Paulo. We are not new buyers arriving at harvest — we are the people the growers already know.
